Frequently Asked

What's the difference between Story Mode and Stats Mode?

Story Mode tells each athlete's clues first-person, in their own voice. Stats Mode gives you the same four clues as plain facts and figures. Switch anytime by tapping the mode badge on the home screen — it just flips the presentation, not the athletes or the difficulty.

How does a match work?

Spin the wheel or tap a category. Each round gives you an athlete and up to four clues — Origin, First, Legacy, Signature — revealed one at a time. Guess as soon as you're sure; earlier guesses are worth more stamps, but wait too long and your opponent might jump in first. Best of three rounds wins the match.

Who am I playing against?

The Panel — twelve analysts, each with their own style. Some guess early and often, some wait for the obscure clue before saying a word. Who you face next rotates automatically through everyone you've unlocked; you can browse the whole roster and your record against each of them from the Panel screen, but you can't hand-pick who's up next.

How do ranks work?

You earn stamps for every correct guess. Stamps move you up the ranks — Bandwagon, Season Ticket, Film Study, Draft Analyst, Hall of Fame Voter, GOAT Status — and each new rank opens up new analysts on the Panel.

What's Friends & Family?

A mode where you load in your own people — nicknames, inside jokes, whoever your circle would actually recognize — and play a private match built entirely from your own list. You'll need at least a handful of entries saved before a match can start.

I found a bug, or a clue looks wrong. What do I do?

Email us below with what you saw and, if you can, a screenshot — we read every message and fix real issues quickly.
